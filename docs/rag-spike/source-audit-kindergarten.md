## PDFBox exceptions

PDFBox attaches visible superscript amendment footnote numbers to adjacent words in three rows. The source was visually checked and the curriculum text was transcribed without those footnote markers. The rows and exact locations are declared in `text-transcriptions.jsonl` and `manifest.json`:

- `kg-math-measure-45`, PDF 66 / printed 78 / age 4–5 (footnote 71)
- `kg-math-measure-56`, PDF 66 / printed 78 / age 5–6 (footnote 72)
- `kg-lang-recall-56`, PDF 69 / printed 81 / age 5–6 (footnote 73)

The other 131 outcomes passed the declared PDFBox age-column check. A source hash or fragment mismatch fails extraction.

## Independent review and limits

Independent source reviewers passed the expanded science/social, math, listening, speaking/narration and early-literacy batches. Reviews checked the rendered source, age-column assignment, one-outcome chunk boundaries, metadata and transcription. The review agents did not provide specialist curriculum approval.

Passing source verification proves that the transcribed outcomes are present in the supplied PDF. It does not establish pedagogical suitability, retrieval quality, completeness beyond the declared cognitive/language scope, or production readiness. No embeddings were ingested and no runtime scope was published in this audit.

Evidence is generated by `CurriculumPdfExtractorTest` and the extraction-only `rag-spike.ps1 -Mode Extract -Dataset kindergarten` mode. The latest report is stored at `docs/rag-spike/evidence/kindergarten-source-verification.json`.
